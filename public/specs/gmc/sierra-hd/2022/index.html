Fluids & capacities

Engine Coolant

50/50 mixture of clean, drinkable water and use only DEX-COOL coolant. See Cooling System 0 316.

Applies to: Engine Coolant

Source: 2022 GMC Sierra Denali 2500HD and 3500HD Owner Manual, page 400

Engine Cooling System*

Engine Oil

Engine oil meeting the dexos1 specification of the proper SAE viscosity grade. ACDelco dexos1 full synthetic is recommended. See Engine Oil 0 311.

Applies to: Engine Oil

Source: 2022 GMC Sierra Denali 2500HD and 3500HD Owner Manual, page 400

Maintenance schedule

Normal service schedule

Use this for ordinary driving.

ServiceWhen
Replace passenger compartment air filter. (2)

Source: 2022 GMC Sierra Denali 2500HD and 3500HD Owner Manual, page 394

Every 22,500 mi(36,000 km)
Rotate tires and perform Required Services. Check engine oil level and oil life percentage. Change engine oil and filter, if needed. Check engine air filter life percentage and status. Change engine air filter, if needed. (1)

Source: 2022 GMC Sierra Denali 2500HD and 3500HD Owner Manual, page 394

Every 7,500 mi(12,000 km)

Severe service schedule

Use this if you tow, idle a lot, drive short trips in the cold, or run in dust.

ServiceWhen

Where to find it

Instrument Panel Fuse Block (Left) The left instrument panel fuse block access door is on the driver side edge of the instrument panel.

Pull off the cover to access the fuse block.
