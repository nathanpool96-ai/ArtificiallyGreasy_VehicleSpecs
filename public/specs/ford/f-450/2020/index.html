Oil life & reset procedures

Oil change indicator reset

Before you start

  • Use the information display controls on the steering wheel to reset the oil change indicator.
  • From the main menu scroll to:

Steps

  1. Press the down arrow button, then from this menu scroll to the following message. [Truck Info]
  2. Press the OK button. [Maintenance Monitor]
  3. Press the down arrow button, then from this menu scroll to the following message. [Oil Life: xxx%]
  4. Press the OK button. [Oil Life]
  5. Press and hold the OK button until the instrument cluster displays the following message. Oil Life: 100% [Oil Life Hold OK to Reset]

You’ll know it worked: When the oil change indicator resets the instrument cluster displays 100%.

Source: Owner’s Manual (PDF), page 352

Fuse box locations & fuse chart

Passenger Compartment Fuse Panel

Where to find it

Passenger Compartment Fuse Panel The fuse panel is in the right-hand side of the passenger footwell behind a trim panel.

To remove the trim panel, pull it toward you and swing it away from the side.
