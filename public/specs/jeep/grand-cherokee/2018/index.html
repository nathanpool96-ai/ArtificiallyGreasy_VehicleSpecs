Oil life & reset procedures

Engine oil life / oil change indicator reset

Oil Life Reset

Applies to: Oil Life Reset

Before you start

  • Without pushing the brake pedal, place the ignition in the ON/RUN mode (do not start the engine).

Steps

  1. Without pushing the brake pedal, place the ignition in the ON/RUN mode (do not start the engine).
  2. Navigate to Oil Life submenu in Vehicle Info in the instrument cluster display.
  3. Push and hold the OK button until the gauge resets to 100%.

Source: 2018 Jeep Grand Cherokee Owner's Manual, page 138

Engine oil life / oil change indicator reset

Secondary Method For Oil Change Reset Procedure

Applies to: Secondary Method For Oil Change Reset Procedure

Before you start

  • Without pushing the brake pedal, push and release the ENGINE START/STOP button and place the ignition to the ON/RUN position (do not start the engine).

Steps

  1. Without pushing the brake pedal, push and release the ENGINE START/STOP button and place the ignition to the ON/RUN position (do not start the engine).
  2. Fully press the accelerator pedal, slowly, three times within ten seconds.
  3. Without pushing the brake pedal, push and release the ENGINE START/STOP button once to return the igni- tion to the OFF/LOCK position.

If the indicator message illuminates when you start the vehicle, the oil change indicator system did not reset.

Source: 2018 Jeep Grand Cherokee Owner's Manual, page 138
