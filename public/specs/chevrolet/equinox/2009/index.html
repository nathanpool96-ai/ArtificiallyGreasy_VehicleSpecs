Oil life & reset procedures

Engine oil life system reset

Before you start

  • with the engine off.

Steps

  1. Turn the ignition key to ON/RUN with the engine off.
  2. Fully press and release the accelerator pedal three times within ﬁve seconds. The change engine oil light will ﬂash while the system is resetting.
  3. When the light stops ﬂashing, turn the key to LOCK/OFF.

Source: 2009 Chevrolet Equinox Owner Manual, page 5-19
