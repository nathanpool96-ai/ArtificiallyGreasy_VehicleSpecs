Oil life & reset procedures

Engine oil life system reset

Before you start

  • Reset the system whenever the engine oil is changed so that the system can calculate the next engine oil change.

Steps

  1. Using the DIC controls on the right side of the steering wheel, display OIL LIFE on the DIC. See Driver Information Center (DIC) 0 145. When remaining oil life is low, the CHANGE ENGINE OIL SOON message will appear on the display.
  2. Press and hold SEL on the DIC controls for a few seconds to clear the CHANGE ENGINE OIL SOON message and reset the oil life at 100%.
  3. Disconnect the electrical connector (2) and the connector harness from the cover.

You’ll know it worked: If the CHANGE ENGINE OIL SOON message is not on, the system is reset.

Use of the incorrect automatic transmission fluid may damage the vehicle, and the damage may not be covered by the vehicle warranty.

Source: 2018 Cadillac Escalade Owner Manual, page 271
