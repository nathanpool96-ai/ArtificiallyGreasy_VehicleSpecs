Oil life & reset procedures

Reset the maintenance data (U.S.A. only)

Before you start

  • After the required maintenance is performed according to the maintenance schedule, please reset the maintenance data.

Steps

  1. Switch the display to the trip meter “A” (→P. 198) when the hybrid system is operating.
  2. Turn the “POWER” switch off.
  3. While pressing the MPH or km/h button (→P. 199), turn the “POWER” switch to ON mode (do not start the hybrid system because otherwise the reset mode will be canceled). Continue to press and hold the button until the trip meter displays “000000” and the indicator stops flashing to indicate that the reset is complete.

You’ll know it worked: While pressing the MPH or km/h button (→P. 199), turn the “POWER” switch to ON mode (do not start the hybrid system because otherwise the reset mode will be canceled). Continue to press and hold the button until the trip meter displays “000000” and the indicator stops flashing to indicate that the reset is complete.

Source: Toyota 2014 Prius V Owners Manual (OM47840U), page 356
