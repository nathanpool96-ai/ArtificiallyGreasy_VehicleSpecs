Oil life & reset procedures

Reset the maintenance data (U.S.A. only)

Before you start

  • After the required mainteance is performed according to the maintenance schedule, please reset the maintenance data.

Steps

  1. Turn the engine switch off with the odometer reading shown. (→P. 137)
  2. Turn the engine switch to the ON position while holding down the trip meter reset button.
  3. Continue to press and hold the button until the odometer displays 000000.

You’ll know it worked: Continue to press and hold the button until the odometer displays 000000.

Source: Toyota 2010 FJ Cruiser Owners Manual (OM35A25U), page 284
