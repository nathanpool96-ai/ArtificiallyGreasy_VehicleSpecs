Oil life & reset procedures

Engine oil life monitor reset

Steps

  1. Tap on the ODO/TRIP button to toggle the display between the odometer and trip odometer.
  2. Tap on the ODO/TRIP button again to toggle the display between the trip odometer and OIL LIFE XX% display.
  3. Press and hold the ODO/TRIP button for ten seconds or more to display OIL LIFE 100%. Your oil life is now reset.
  4. Tap on the ODO/TRIP button to return the display to odometer.

Source: Owner’s Manual Printing 2 (PDF), page 16
