Maintenance schedule

Normal service schedule

Use this for ordinary driving.

ServiceWhen
Change automatic transmission fluid and filter.

Applies to: 3.2L, 3.5L and 3.7L Engines

Source: Owner’s Manual Printing 2 (PDF), page 438

Every 150,000 mi(240,000 km)
Change diesel foam air filter.

Applies to: 3.2L, 3.5L and 3.7L Engines

Source: Owner’s Manual Printing 2 (PDF), page 437

Every 45,000 mi(70,000 km)
Change engine oil and filter.

Applies to: 3.2L, 3.5L and 3.7L Engines

Source: Owner’s Manual Printing 2 (PDF), page 437

At every oil change interval as indicated by the information display*
Replace engine air filter.

Applies to: 3.2L, 3.5L and 3.7L Engines

Source: Owner’s Manual Printing 2 (PDF), page 437

Every 30,000 mi(48,000 km)

Severe service schedule

Use this if you tow, idle a lot, drive short trips in the cold, or run in dust.

ServiceWhen
Change engine oil and filter as indicated by the information display and perform services listed in the Normal Scheduled Maintenance chart.

Applies to: 3.2L, 3.5L and 3.7L Engines; Towing a trailer or using a car-top carrier · Towing a trailer or using a car-top carrier

Source: Owner’s Manual Printing 2 (PDF), page 438

As required
Change engine oil and filter.

Applies to: 3.2L, 3.5L and 3.7L Engines; Operating in dusty or sandy conditions (such as unpaved or dusty roads) · Operating in dusty or sandy conditions (such as unpaved or dusty roads)

Source: Owner’s Manual Printing 2 (PDF), page 439

Every 5,000 mi(8,000 km)
Replace engine air filter.

Applies to: 3.2L, 3.5L and 3.7L Engines; Extensive idling or low-speed driving for long distances, as in heavy commercial use (such as delivery, taxi, patrol car or livery) · Extensive idling or low-speed driving for long distances, as in heavy commercial use (such as delivery, taxi, patrol car or livery)

Source: Owner’s Manual Printing 2 (PDF), page 439
