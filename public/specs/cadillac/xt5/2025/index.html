Fuse box locations & fuse chart

Instrument Panel Fuse Block

Where to find it

Instrument Panel Fuse Block The instrument panel fuse block is in the center console between the driver and passenger seats.

open the fuse panel door, or remove the panel from the passenger side by pulling it out or see your dealer.

Source: 2025 Cadillac XT5 Owner Manual, page 287

Rear Compartment Fuse Block

Where to find it

Rear Compartment Fuse Block The rear compartment fuse block is in the cargo area, on the driver side of the vehicle behind the lower trim panel.

open, turn the latch with a flat-bladed tool and pull the trim panel from the edges to fold it down.

Source: 2025 Cadillac XT5 Owner Manual, page 289
