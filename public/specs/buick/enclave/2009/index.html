Oil life & reset procedures

Engine oil life system reset

Before you start

  • Turn the ignition to ON/RUN, with the engine off.

Steps

  1. Turn the ignition to ON/RUN, with the engine off.
  2. Press the vehicle information button until OIL LIFE REMAINING displays.
  3. Press and hold the set/reset button until 100% is displayed. You will hear three chimes and the CHANGE ENGINE OIL SOON message will go off.
  4. Turn the key to LOCK/OFF.
  5. Inspect or replace the air ﬁlter element.
  6. Reverse Steps 1 through 3 to reinstall the cover and reconnect the electrical connector.

Operating the engine with the air cleaner/ﬁlter off can cause you or others to be burned.

Source: 2009 Buick Enclave Owner Manual, page 5-14

Instrument Panel Fuse Block

Where to find it

Instrument Panel Fuse Block The instrument panel fuse block is located under the instrument panel on the passenger side of the vehicle.

Pull down on the cover to access the fuse block.
