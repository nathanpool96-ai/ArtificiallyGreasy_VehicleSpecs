Oil life & reset procedures

Engine oil life system reset

Steps

  1. Turn the key to the ON/RUN position without starting the engine.
  2. Press the INFO button on the Driver Information Center (DIC) until OIL LIFE REMAINING is displayed. See DIC Operation and Displays on page 4-62.
  3. Press and hold the INFO RESET button until 100% is displayed. This resets the oil life indicator.
  4. Turn the key to LOCK/OFF.

Source: 2010 Cadillac DTS Owner Manual, page 6-17
