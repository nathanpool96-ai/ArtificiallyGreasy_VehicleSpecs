Automatic Transmission

Source: 2026 GMC Yukon Owner Manual, page 394

Engine Coolant

50/50 mixture of clean, drinkable water and use only DEX-COOL Coolant. See Cooling System 3 316.

Applies to: Engine Coolant

Source: 2026 GMC Yukon Owner Manual, page 394

Engine Oil

Engine oil meeting the dexos1 specification of the proper SAE viscosity grade. ACDelco dexos1 full synthetic is recommended. See Engine Oil 3 312.

Applies to: Engine Oil

Fuse box locations & fuse chart

Engine Compartment Fuse Block

Where to find it

Engine Compartment Fuse Block The engine compartment fuse block is in the engine compartment, on the driver side of the vehicle.

Lift the cover to access the fuse block.

Source: 2026 GMC Yukon Owner Manual, page 330

Instrument Panel Fuse Block

Where to find it

Instrument Panel Fuse Block The right instrument panel fuse block access door is on the passenger side edge of the instrument panel.

pressor K64 STRTR MTR - Starter Motor K70 STRTR PINION - Starter Pinion K77 PWR/TRN - Powertrain Instrument Panel Fuse Block The right instrument panel fuse block access door is on the passenger side edge of the instrument panel.

Source: 2026 GMC Yukon Owner Manual, page 335

Rear Compartment Fuse Block

Where to find it

Rear Compartment Fuse Block The rear compartment fuse block is behind the access panel on the left side of the compartment.

Pull the panel out by grabbing the finger access slot at the rear edge.

Source: 2026 GMC Yukon Owner Manual, page 339
