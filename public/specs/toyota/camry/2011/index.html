Oil life & reset procedures

Reset the maintenance data (U.S.A. only)

Before you start

  • After the required maintenance is performed according to the maintenance schedule, please reset the maintenance data.

Steps

  1. Turn the “ENGINE START STOP” switch or engine switch OFF with the trip meter A reading shown. (→P. 158)
  2. While pressing the trip meter reset button (→P. 150), turn the “ENGINE START STOP” switch or engine switch ON.
  3. Without multi-information display
  4. Continue to press and hold the knob until the trip meter displays 000000.
  5. With multi-information display
  6. Continue to press and hold the knob until “COMPLETE” appears on the multi-information display.

You’ll know it worked: Continue to press and hold the knob until the trip meter displays 000000.

Source: Toyota 2011 Camry Owners Manual (OM33897U), page 360
