Maintenance schedule

Normal service schedule

Use this for ordinary driving.

ServiceWhen
Replace engine air cleaner filter. (3)

Source: 2015 GMC Sierra Owner Manual, page 11-5

Every 45,000 mi(72,000 km)
Replace passenger compartment air filter. (1)

Source: 2015 GMC Sierra Owner Manual, page 11-5

Every 22,500 mi(36,000 km)
Rotate tires and perform Required Services. Check engine oil level and oil life percentage. Change engine oil and filter, if needed.

Source: 2015 GMC Sierra Owner Manual, page 11-5

Every 7,500 mi(12,000 km)

Severe service schedule

Use this if you tow, idle a lot, drive short trips in the cold, or run in dust.

ServiceWhen
Change automatic transmission fluid and filter.

Engine Compartment Fuse Block

Where to find it

engine compartment fuse block is in the engine compartment, on the driver side of the vehicle.

Lift the cover to access the fuse block.

Source: 2015 GMC Sierra Denali Owner Manual, page 10-41
