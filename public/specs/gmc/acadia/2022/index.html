Oil life & reset procedures

Engine oil life system reset

Before you start

  • Reset the system whenever the engine oil is changed so that the system can calculate the next engine oil change.

Steps

  1. Display the REMAINING OIL LIFE on the DIC. See Driver Information Center (DIC) (Base Level) 0 116 or Driver Information Center (DIC) (Midlevel and Uplevel) 0 118.
  2. Press and hold V on the DIC while the Oil Life display is active. The oil life will change to 100%.
  3. Press p to move to the Reset/Disable display area. Select Reset then press V.
  4. Press V to confirm to reset.

You’ll know it worked: The oil life will change to 100%.

Use of the incorrect automatic transmission fluid may damage the vehicle, and the damage may not be covered by the vehicle warranty.

Source: 2022 GMC Acadia/ Acadia Denali Owner Manual, page 277
