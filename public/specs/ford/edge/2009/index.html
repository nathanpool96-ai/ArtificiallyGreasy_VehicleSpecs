Fuse box locations & fuse chart

Passenger compartment fuse panel

Where to find it

Passenger compartment fuse panel The fuse panel is located behind a trim panel on the left side of the driver's footwell near the parking brake.

To remove the trim panel, slide the release lever to the right then pull the trim panel out.

Source: Owner’s Manual Printing 1 (PDF), page 242
