Fluids & capacities

Automatic Transmission (6-Speed Transmission)

DEXRON-VI Automatic Transmission Fluid.

Applies to: Automatic Transmission (6-Speed Transmission)

Source: 2021 GMC Sierra Owner Manual, page 431

Automatic Transmission (8-Speed Transmission)

DEXRON-HP Automatic Transmission Fluid.

Applies to: Automatic Transmission (8-Speed Transmission)

Source: 2021 GMC Sierra Owner Manual, page 431

Engine Coolant

50/50 mixture of clean, drinkable water and use only DEX-COOL coolant. See Cooling System 3 342.

Applies to: Engine Coolant

Source: 2021 GMC Sierra Owner Manual, page 431

Engine Oil

Engine oil meeting the dexos1 specification of the proper SAE viscosity grade. ACDelco dexos1 full synthetic is recommended. See Engine Oil 3 337.

Applies to: Engine Oil

Source: 2021 GMC Sierra Owner Manual, page 431

Where to find it

engine compartment fuse block is in the engine compartment, on the passenger side of the vehicle.

Lift the cover to access the fuse block.

Instrument Panel Fuse Block (Left)

Where to find it

Instrument Panel Fuse Block (Left) The left instrument panel fuse block access door is on the driver side edge of the instrument panel.

Pull off the cover to access the fuse block.

Source: 2021 GMC Sierra Owner Manual, page 365

Instrument Panel Fuse Block (Right) The right instrument panel fuse block access door is on the passenger side edge of the instrument panel.
