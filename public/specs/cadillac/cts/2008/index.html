  2. Press and hold the SET/RESET button on the DIC for more than ﬁve seconds. The oil life will change to 100%.
  3. Align cover over the oil ﬁll tube.
  4. Press cover down ﬁrmly on ball stud at the right front corner.
  5. Install oil ﬁll cap (A).
  6. Install the cross car brace.
