Oil life & reset procedures

Engine oil life monitor reset

Printing 1

Steps

  1. Press and hold the RESET control for 2 seconds and release. Oil life is set to 100% and “OIL LIFE XXX% HOLD RESET IF NEW OIL” is displayed.
  2. Press and hold the RESET control for 3 seconds and release. Oil life is set to 100% and “OIL LIFE START VALUE SET TO XXX%” is displayed. 2006 Crown Victoria (cro) Owners Guide (post-2002-fmt) USA (fus)

Source: Owner’s Manual Printing 1 (PDF), page 81
