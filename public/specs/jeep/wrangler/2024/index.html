All engines

Fluid

Use only Mopar® ZF 8 & 9 Speed Automatic Transmission Fluid (ATF) or equivalent. Failure to use the correct fluid may affect the function or performance of your transmission.

Applies to: Automatic Transmission - If Equipped

Source: 2024 Jeep Wrangler Owner's Manual, page 347

Fluid

We recommend using Mopar® ATF+4 Automatic Transmission Fluid.

Applies to: Manual Transmission - If Equipped

Source: 2024 Jeep Wrangler Owner's Manual, page 347

Fluid

We recommend using Mopar® Antifreeze/Coolant 10 Year/150,000 Mile (240,000 km) Formula OAT (Organic Additive Technology) or equivalent meeting the requirements of the manufacturer Material Standard MS.90032.

Applies to: Battery, and Power Electric Coolant

Source: 2024 Jeep Wrangler Owner's Manual, page 346
