Oil life & reset procedures

Engine oil life system reset

Before you start

  • Reset the system whenever the engine oil is changed so that the system can calculate the next engine oil change.
  • with the engine off.

Steps

  1. Using the DIC controls on the right side of the steering wheel, display REMAINING OIL LIFE on the DIC. See Driver Information Center (DIC) 0 137. When remaining oil life is low, the CHANGE ENGINE OIL SOON message will appear on the display. See Engine Oil Messages 0 146.
  2. Press SEL on the DIC controls and hold SEL down for a few seconds to clear the CHANGE ENGINE OIL SOON message and reset the oil life at 100%.
  3. Pull out the filter.
  4. Inspect or replace the engine air cleaner/filter.
  5. Reverse Steps 1-3 to reinstall the filter cover housing.

You’ll know it worked: If the CHANGE ENGINE OIL SOON message is not on, the system is reset.

Source: 2017 Cadillac XTS Owner Manual, page 252
