Oil life & reset procedures

Engine oil life monitor reset

Steps

  1. Select this function from the SETUP control for current display mode.
  2. Press and release the RESET control to display “IF NEW OIL HOLD RESET”.
  3. Press and hold the RESET control to display OIL LIFE SET TO 100%. Your oil life is now reset. To set the oil life to 100% without system warnings displayed: Press SETUP to display “PRESS RESET FOR SYS CHECK”. 1. Press RESET to start the System Check which will display “OIL LIFE XX% RESET IF NEW”. 2. Press and hold the RESET until “IF NEW OIL HOLD RESET” is displayed, then release. 2007 Five Hundred (500) Owners Guide (post-2002-fmt) USA (fus)
