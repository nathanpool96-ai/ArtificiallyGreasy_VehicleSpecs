Oil life & reset procedures

Engine oil life / oil change indicator reset

Your vehicle is equipped with an engine oil change indi-

Applies to: Your vehicle is equipped with an engine oil change indi-

Before you start

  • Without pressing the brake pedal, push the ENGINE START/STOP button and place the ignition in the ON/RUN position (do not start the engine).

Steps

  1. Without pressing the brake pedal, push the ENGINE START/STOP button and place the ignition in the ON/RUN position (do not start the engine).
  2. Push and release the down arrow button to scroll downward through the main menu to “Vehicle Info.”
  3. Push and release the right arrow button to access the ”Oil Life” screen.
  4. Push and hold the OK button to reset oil life. If con- ditions are met, the gauge and numeric display will update to show 100%. If conditions are not met a pop-up message of “To reset oil life engine must be off with ignition in run” will be displayed (for five seconds), and the user will remain at the Oil Life screen.
  5. Push and release the up or down arrow button to exit the submenu screen.

Source: 2020 Ram 1500 Owner's Manual, page 92
