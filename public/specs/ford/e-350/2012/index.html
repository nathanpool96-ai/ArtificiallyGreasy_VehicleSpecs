Oil life & reset procedures

Engine oil life monitor reset

Steps

  1. Press and release the SELECT/RESET stem to display “OIL LIFE XXX% HOLD RESET = NEW”.
  2. Press and hold the SELECT/RESET stem for two seconds and release to reset the oil life to 100%.
  3. Once “OIL LIFE SET TO XXX%” is displayed, release and press the SELECT/RESET stem to change the oil life start value. Each release and press will reduce the value by 10%. UNITS ENG / METRIC Displays the current units English or Metric. Press and hold the SELECT/RESET stem to change from English to Metric. Press the SELECT/RESET stem for the next setup menu item or wait for more than four seconds to return to the info menu. AUTOLOCK (if equipped) This feature automatically locks all vehicle doors when the vehicle is shifted into any gear and when the vehicle is in motion over 13 mph (20 km/h) or higher. Press and hold the SELECT/RESET stem to turn this feature on or off. Press the SELECT/RESET stem for the next setup menu item or wait for more than four seconds to return to the info menu. 2012 Econoline (eco) Owners Guide, 1st Printing USA (fus)

Source: Owner’s Manual Printing 1 (PDF), page 23
