Oil life & reset procedures

Engine oil life system reset

Before you start

  • Reset the system whenever the engine oil is changed so that the system can calculate the next engine oil change.

Steps

  1. Using the DIC buttons, display REMAINING OIL LIFE on the DIC. See Driver Information Center (DIC) 0 137.
  2. Press and hold SEL to clear the CHANGE ENGINE OIL SOON message and reset the oil life at 100%.
  3. Pull out the air cleaner/filter.
  4. Inspect or replace the air cleaner/filter.
  5. Reverse Steps 1-3 to reinstall the air cleaner/filter cover.

You’ll know it worked: If the CHANGE ENGINE OIL SOON message is not on, the system is reset.

Source: 2018 Cadillac CTS and Cadillac CTS-V Owner Manual, page 266
