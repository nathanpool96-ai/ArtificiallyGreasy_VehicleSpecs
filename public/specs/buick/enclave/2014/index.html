Oil life & reset procedures

Engine oil life system reset

Before you start

  • Reset the system whenever the engine oil is changed so that the system can calculate the next engine oil change.
  • Turn the ignition to ON/RUN, with the engine off.

Steps

  1. Turn the ignition to ON/RUN, with the engine off.
  2. Press the vehicle information button until OIL LIFE REMAINING displays.
  3. Press and hold the set/reset button until “100%” is displayed. Three chimes sound and the CHANGE ENGINE OIL SOON message goes off.
  4. Turn the key to LOCK/OFF.
  5. Reverse Steps 1-4 to reinstall the housing cover and reconnect the electrical connector to the sensor.

You’ll know it worked: Three chimes sound and the CHANGE ENGINE OIL SOON message goes off.

Use of the incorrect automatic transmission fluid may damage the vehicle, and the damage may not be covered by the vehicle warranty.

Fuse box locations & fuse chart

Engine Compartment Fuse Block

Where to find it

Engine Compartment Fuse Block The underhood fuse block is in the engine compartment, on the passenger side of the vehicle.

Lift the cover for access to the fuse/ relay block.

Source: 2014 Buick Enclave Owner Manual, page 10-30

Instrument Panel Fuse Block

Where to find it

Instrument Panel Fuse Block The instrument panel fuse block is under the instrument panel on the passenger side of the vehicle.

Pull down on the cover to access the fuse block.
