Maintenance schedule

Normal service schedule

Use this for ordinary driving.

ServiceWhen
Change automatic transmission fluid and filter.

Source: Owner’s Manual Printing 3 (PDF), page 400

Every 150,000 mi(240,000 km)
Change engine oil and filter.

Source: Owner’s Manual Printing 2 (PDF), page 396

At every oil change interval as indicated by the information display*
Replace cabin air filter.

Source: Owner’s Manual Printing 3 (PDF), page 400

Every 20,000 mi(32,000 km)
Replace engine air filter.

Source: Owner’s Manual Printing 3 (PDF), page 400

Every 30,000 mi(48,000 km)

Severe service schedule

Use this if you tow, idle a lot, drive short trips in the cold, or run in dust.

ServiceWhen
Change engine oil and filter as indicated by the information display and perform services listed in the Normal Scheduled Maintenance chart.

Applies to: Towing a trailer or using a car-top carrier

Source: Owner’s Manual Printing 2 (PDF), page 398

As required
Change engine oil and filter.

Applies to: Operating in dusty or sandy conditions (such as unpaved or dusty roads)

Source: Owner’s Manual Printing 3 (PDF), page 402

Every 5,000 mi(8,000 km)
Inspect engine air filter. Replace as required.

Applies to: Extensive idling or low-speed driving for long distances, as in heavy commercial use (such as delivery, taxi, patrol car or livery)

Source: Owner’s Manual Printing 3 (PDF), page 401

Every 15,000 mi(24,000 km)
Replace cabin air filter.

Applies to: Extensive idling or low-speed driving for long distances, as in heavy commercial use (such as delivery, taxi, patrol car or livery)

Source: Owner’s Manual Printing 2 (PDF), page 398

Inspect frequently, service as required

Fuse box locations & fuse chart

Passenger Compartment Fuse Panel

Where to find it

Passenger Compartment Fuse Panel The fuse panel is in the right-hand side of the passenger footwell behind a trim panel and plastic key code card.

To remove the trim panel, lift it from the rear retaining hooks, while pulling it toward you and swinging it away from the side.

Source: Owner’s Manual Printing 1 (PDF), page 206
