Maintenance schedule

Normal service schedule

Use this for ordinary driving.

ServiceWhen
Change automatic transmission fluid. Change filter if serviceable.

Source: 2014 GMC Yukon Owner Manual, page 11-5

97,500 mi
Replace engine air cleaner filter. (2)

Source: 2014 GMC Yukon Owner Manual, page 11-5

Every 45,000 mi(72,000 km)
Rotate tires and perform Required Services. Check engine oil level and oil life percentage. Change engine oil and filter, if needed.

Source: 2014 GMC Yukon Owner Manual, page 11-5

Every 7,500 mi(12,000 km)
