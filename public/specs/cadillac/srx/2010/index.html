Oil life & reset procedures

Engine oil life system reset

Before you start

  • Reset the system whenever the engine oil is changed so that the system can calculate the next engine oil change.
  • Turn the ignition to ON/RUN with the engine off.

Steps

  1. Using the DIC MENU button and thumbwheel on the turn signal lever, display “Oil Life Remaining” on the DIC. See Driver Information Center (DIC) on page 4‑29 and Engine Oil Messages on page 4‑36.
  2. Press the SET button to reset the oil life at 100%.

You’ll know it worked: If the “Change Engine Oil Soon” message is not on, the system is reset.

Source: 2010 Cadillac SRX Owner Manual, page 9-12

Instrument Panel Fuse Block

Where to find it

Instrument Panel Fuse Block The instrument panel fuse block is located in the center console between the driver and passenger seats.

open the fuse panel door from the passenger side by pulling it out.

Rear Compartment Fuse Block

Where to find it

Rear Compartment Fuse Block The rear compartment fuse block is located in the cargo area, on the driver side of the vehicle behind the lower trim panel.

open, turn the latch with a flat bladed tool and pull the trim panel from the edges to fold it down.
