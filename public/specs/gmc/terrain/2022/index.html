Oil life & reset procedures

Engine oil life system reset

Before you start

  • Reset the system whenever the engine oil is changed so that the system can calculate the next engine oil change.

Steps

  1. Display REMAINING OIL LIFE on the DIC menu. See Driver Information Center (DIC) (Base and Midlevel) 0 107 or Driver Information Center (DIC) (Uplevel and Denali) 0 111.
  2. Press and hold V for several seconds while the Oil Life display is active to reset the Oil Life system. 3. REMAINING OIL LIFE 100% will be displayed when the oil life system is successfully reset.
  3. Press p on the steering wheel to move to the Reset/Disable display area. Select Reset then press V.
  4. Press V to confirm the reset.

You’ll know it worked: If the display changes to 100%, the system is reset.

Use of the incorrect automatic transmission fluid may damage the vehicle, and the damage may not be covered by the vehicle warranty.

Source: 2022 GMC Terrain/Terrain Denali Owner Manual, page 278
