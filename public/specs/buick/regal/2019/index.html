Oil life & reset procedures

Engine oil life system reset

Before you start

  • Reset the system whenever the engine oil is changed so that the system can calculate the next engine oil change.

Steps

  1. Using the DIC controls on the right side of the steering wheel, display REMAINING OIL LIFE on the DIC. See Driver Information Center (DIC) 0 144. When remaining oil life is low, the CHANGE ENGINE OIL SOON message will appear on the display.
  2. Press V on the DIC controls and hold down for a few seconds to clear the CHANGE ENGINE OIL SOON message and reset the oil life at 100%.
  3. Pull out the filter.
  4. Inspect or replace the engine air cleaner/filter.

You’ll know it worked: The system is reset when the CHANGE ENGINE OIL SOON message is off.

Use of the incorrect automatic transmission fluid may damage the vehicle, and the damage may (Continued) Vehicle Care 255 Caution (Continued) not be covered by the vehicle warranty.
