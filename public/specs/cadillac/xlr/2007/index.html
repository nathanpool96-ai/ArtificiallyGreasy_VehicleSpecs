Instrument Panel Fuse Block

Where to find it

Instrument Panel Fuse Block The panel fuse block is located on the passenger's side of the vehicle, under the instrument panel and under the toe-board.

Remove the carpet and toe-board covering to access the fuse block by pulling at the top of each corner of the panel.

Source: 2007 Cadillac XLR and Cadillac XLR V Owners Manual, page 453
