Oil life & reset procedures

Engine oil life system reset

Before you start

  • Reset the system whenever the engine oil is changed so that the system can calculate the next engine oil change.

Steps

  1. Using the DIC MENU button and thumbwheel, scroll until you reach Remaining Oil Life on the DIC.
  2. Press the SET/CLR button to reset the oil life at 100%. When prompted, use the thumbwheel to highlight YES or NO. Press the SET/CLEAR button to confirm.

Source: 2012 Chevrolet Sonic Owner Manual, page 10-12
