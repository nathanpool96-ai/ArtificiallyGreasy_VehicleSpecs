Fluids & capacities

Automatic Transmission (6-Speed Transmission)

DEXRON-VI Automatic Transmission Fluid.

Applies to: Automatic Transmission (6-Speed Transmission)

Source: 2022 GMC Sierra/ Sierra Denali LTD Owner Manual, page 433

Automatic Transmission (8-Speed Transmission)

DEXRON-HP Automatic Transmission Fluid.

Applies to: Automatic Transmission (8-Speed Transmission)

Source: 2022 GMC Sierra/ Sierra Denali LTD Owner Manual, page 433

Engine Coolant

50/50 mixture of clean, drinkable water and use only DEX-COOL coolant. See Cooling System 3 345.

Applies to: Engine Coolant

Source: 2022 GMC Sierra/ Sierra Denali LTD Owner Manual, page 433

Engine Oil

Engine oil meeting the dexos1 specification of the proper SAE viscosity grade. ACDelco dexos1 full synthetic is recommended. See Engine Oil 3 340.

Applies to: Engine Oil

Source: 2022 GMC Sierra/ Sierra Denali LTD Owner Manual, page 434
