Auxiliary relay box

Where to find it

Auxiliary relay box (Vehicles equipped with 4x4) The relay box is located on the front right fender well.

Source: Owner’s Manual Printing 3 (PDF), page 239

Passenger compartment fuse panel

Where to find it

Passenger compartment fuse panel The fuse panel is located below the instrument panel on the driver's side.

To remove a fuse use the fuse puller tool provided on the fuse panel box.

Source: Owner’s Manual Printing 3 (PDF), page 230
