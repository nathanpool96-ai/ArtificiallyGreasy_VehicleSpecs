Oil life & reset procedures

Engine oil life system reset

Before you start

  • Turn the ignition to ON/RUN, with the engine off.

Steps

  1. Turn the ignition to ON/RUN, with the engine off. The vehicle must be in P (Park) to access this display. Press the trip odometer reset stem until OIL LIFE REMAINING displays.
  2. Press and hold the trip odometer reset stem until OIL LIFE REMAINING shows 100%. Three chimes sound and the CHANGE ENGINE OIL SOON message goes off.
  3. Turn the key to LOCK/OFF.
  4. Turn the key to LOCK/OFF.

You’ll know it worked: Three chimes sound and the CHANGE ENGINE OIL SOON message goes off.

Source: 2010 Chevrolet Traverse Owner Manual, page 10-10

Fuse box locations & fuse chart

Engine Compartment Fuse Block

Where to find it

Engine Compartment Fuse Block The underhood fuse block is located in the engine compartment, on the passenger side of the vehicle.

Lift the cover for access to the fuse/ relay block.

Source: 2010 Chevrolet Traverse Owner Manual, page 10-30

Instrument Panel Fuse Block

Where to find it

Instrument Panel Fuse Block The instrument panel fuse block is located under the instrument panel on the passenger side of the vehicle.

Pull down on the cover to access the fuse block.

Source: 2010 Chevrolet Traverse Owner Manual, page 10-33
