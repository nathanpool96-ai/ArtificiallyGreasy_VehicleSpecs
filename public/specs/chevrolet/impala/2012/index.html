Oil life & reset procedures

Engine oil life system reset

Before you start

  • Reset the system whenever the engine oil is changed so that the system can calculate the next engine oil change.

Steps

  1. Display OIL LIFE REMAINING on the DIC.
  2. Press and hold the SET/RESET button on the DIC for more than five seconds. The oil life will change to 100%.
  3. Pull out the filter.
  4. Inspect or replace the engine air cleaner/filter.
  5. Lower the filter cover housing toward the engine.

You’ll know it worked: The oil life will change to 100%.

Source: 2012 Chevrolet Impala Owner Manual, page 10-10
