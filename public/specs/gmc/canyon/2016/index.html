Oil life & reset procedures

Engine oil life system reset

Before you start

  • Reset the system whenever the engine oil is changed so that the system can calculate the next engine oil change.
  • Turn the ignition to ON/RUN with the engine off.

Steps

  1. Display OIL LIFE REMAINING on the DIC. See Driver Information Center (DIC) (Base Level) 0 122ii or Driver Information Center (DIC) (Uplevel) 0 125ii. If the vehicle does not have DIC buttons, the vehicle must be in P (Park) to access this display.
  2. Press and hold the V button on the DIC, or the SET/CLR button on the turn signal lever if the vehicle does not have DIC buttons, for several seconds. The oil life will change to 100%.
  3. Display OIL LIFE REMAINING on the DIC. If the display shows 100%, the system is reset.

You’ll know it worked: The oil life will change to 100%.

Source: 2016 GMC Canyon Owner Manual, page 265

Fuse box locations & fuse chart

Engine Compartment Fuse Block

Where to find it

engine compartment fuse block is in the engine compartment, on the driver side of the vehicle.

Lift the cover to access the fuse block.

Source: 2016 GMC Canyon Owner Manual, page 285

Instrument Panel Fuse Block

Where to find it

Instrument Panel Fuse Block The instrument panel fuse block is behind the passenger side cowl side trim panel.

Remove the plastic nut at the front of the cover, then pull the cover away from the trim panel to access the fuse block.
