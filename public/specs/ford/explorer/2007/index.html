Oil life & reset procedures

Engine oil life monitor reset

Steps

  1. Enter SYSTEM CHECK to display “OIL XXX% RESET IF NEW”.
  2. Press SELECT/RESET control switch to display “HOLD RESET IF NEW OIL”.
  3. Press and hold the SELECT/RESET control switch for more than 2 seconds. Oil life is set to 100% and “OIL CHANGE SET TO 100%” is displayed.

Source: Owner’s Manual Printing 2 (PDF), page 110

Passenger compartment fuse panel

Where to find it

Passenger compartment fuse panel The fuse panel is located below the instrument panel on the driver's side.

To remove a fuse use the fuse puller tool provided on the fuse panel box.
