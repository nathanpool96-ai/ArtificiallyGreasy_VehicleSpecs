Oil life & reset procedures

Engine oil life system reset

Before you start

  • Reset the system whenever the engine oil is changed so that the system can calculate the next engine oil change.

Steps

  1. Using the DIC controls on the right side of the steering wheel, display REMAINING OIL LIFE on the DIC. See Driver Information Center (DIC) 0 119. When remaining oil life is low, the CHANGE ENGINE OIL SOON message will appear on the display.
  2. Press SEL on the DIC controls and hold SEL down for a few seconds to clear the CHANGE ENGINE OIL SOON message and reset the oil life at 100%. Be careful not to reset the oil life display accidentally at any time other than after the oil is changed. It cannot be reset accurately until the next oil change.

You’ll know it worked: If the CHANGE ENGINE OIL SOON message is not on, the system is reset.

Use of the incorrect automatic transmission fluid may damage the vehicle, and the damage may not be covered by the vehicle warranty.

Source: 2022 Cadillac XT5 Owner Manual, page 305

Fuse box locations & fuse chart

Engine Compartment Fuse Block

Where to find it

Engine Compartment Fuse Block The underhood fuse block is in the engine compartment, on the driver side of the vehicle.

remove the fuse block cover, press the clips on the cover and lift it straight up.

Source: 2022 Cadillac XT5 Owner Manual, page 322

Instrument Panel Fuse Block

Where to find it

Instrument Panel Fuse Block The instrument panel fuse block is in the center console between the driver and passenger seats.

open the fuse panel door, or remove the panel from the passenger side by pulling it out or see your dealer.

Source: 2022 Cadillac XT5 Owner Manual, page 325

Rear Compartment Fuse Block

Where to find it

Rear Compartment Fuse Block The rear compartment fuse block is in the cargo area, on the driver side of the vehicle behind the lower trim panel.

open, turn the latch with a flat-bladed tool and pull the trim panel from the edges to fold it down.
