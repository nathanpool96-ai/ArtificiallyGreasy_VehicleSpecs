Maintenance schedule

Normal service schedule

Use this for ordinary driving.

ServiceWhen
Change automatic transmission fluid and filter.

Source: Owner’s Manual Printing 3 (PDF), page 503

Every 150,000 mi(240,000 km)
Change engine oil and filter.

Source: Owner’s Manual Printing 3 (PDF), page 502

At every oil change interval as indicated by the information display*
Replace climate-controlled (heated and cooled) seat filter (if equipped).

Source: Owner’s Manual Printing 1 (PDF), page 523

Every 30,000 mi(48,000 km)
Replace engine air filter.

Source: Owner’s Manual Printing 3 (PDF), page 503

Every 30,000 mi(48,000 km)

Severe service schedule

Use this if you tow, idle a lot, drive short trips in the cold, or run in dust.

ServiceWhen
Change engine oil and filter as indicated by the information display and perform services listed in the Normal Scheduled Maintenance chart.

Applies to: Towing a trailer or using a car-top carrier

Source: Owner’s Manual Printing 3 (PDF), page 504

As required
Replace engine air filter.

Applies to: Extensive idling or low-speed driving for long distances, as in heavy commercial use (such as delivery, taxi, patrol car or livery)

Source: Owner’s Manual Printing 3 (PDF), page 504

Inspect frequently, service as required

Passenger Compartment Fuse Panel

Where to find it

Passenger Compartment Fuse Panel The fuse panel is in the right-hand side of the passenger footwell behind a trim panel.

To remove the trim panel, pull it toward you and swing it away from the side.
