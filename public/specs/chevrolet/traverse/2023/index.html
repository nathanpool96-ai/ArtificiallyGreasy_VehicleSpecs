Oil life & reset procedures

Engine oil life system reset

Before you start

  • Reset the system whenever the engine oil is changed so that the system can calculate the next engine oil change.

Steps

  1. Display the OIL LIFE REMAINING on the DIC. See Driver Information Center (DIC) (Base and Midlevel) 0 118 or Driver Information Center (DIC) (Uplevel) 0 120.
  2. Press and hold V for several seconds. The oil life will change to 100%.
  3. Press p to move to the Reset/Disable display area. Select Reset then press V for several seconds.
  4. Press V to confirm the reset.

You’ll know it worked: The oil life will change to 100%.

Use of the incorrect automatic transmission fluid may damage the vehicle, and the damage may not be covered by the vehicle warranty.

Source: 2023 Chevrolet Traverse Owner Manual, page 262

Fuse box locations & fuse chart

Engine Compartment Fuse Block

Where to find it

Engine Compartment Fuse Block The underhood fuse block is in the engine compartment, on the driver side of the vehicle.

remove the fuse block cover, press the clips on the cover and lift it straight up.

Source: 2023 Chevrolet Traverse Owner Manual, page 278

Instrument Panel Fuse Block

Where to find it

Instrument Panel Fuse Block The instrument panel fuse block is inside the center console on the passenger side of the vehicle.

Remove the fuse access door by inserting a coin or tool into the slot of the access door.

Source: 2023 Chevrolet Traverse Owner Manual, page 281

Rear Compartment Fuse Block

Where to find it

Rear Compartment Fuse Block The rear compartment fuse block is behind a trim panel on the driver side of the rear storage compartment.

removed by prying along the top edge of the trim panel at two notched locations.

Source: 2023 Chevrolet Traverse Owner Manual, page 283
