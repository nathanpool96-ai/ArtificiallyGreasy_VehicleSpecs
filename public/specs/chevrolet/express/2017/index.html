Oil life & reset procedures

Engine oil life system reset

Before you start

  • Reset the system whenever the engine oil is changed so that the system can calculate the next engine oil change.
  • with the engine off.

Steps

  1. Turn the ignition key to ON/ RUN with the engine off.
  2. Fully press and release the accelerator pedal slowly three times within five seconds.
  3. Turn the key to LOCK/OFF.
  4. Let the engine run at idle for two minutes or more.

Too much or too little fluid can damage the transmission.

(Continued) fluid could come out and fall on hot engine parts or exhaust system parts, starting a fire.

Source: 2017 Chevrolet Express Owner Manual, page 222
