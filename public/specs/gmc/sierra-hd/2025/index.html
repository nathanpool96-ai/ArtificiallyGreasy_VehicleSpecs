Fluids & capacities

Automatic Transmission

DEXRON ULV Automatic Transmission Fluid.

Applies to: Automatic Transmission

Source: 2025 GMC Sierra /Sierra Denali 2500HD/3500HD Owner Manual, page 402

Engine Coolant

50/50 mixture of clean, drinkable water and use only DEX-COOL coolant. See Cooling System 3 321.

Applies to: Engine Coolant

Source: 2025 GMC Sierra /Sierra Denali 2500HD/3500HD Owner Manual, page 402

Engine Oil

Engine oil meeting the dexos1 specification of the proper SAE viscosity grade. ACDelco dexos1 full synthetic is recommended. See Engine Oil 3 316.

Applies to: Engine Oil

Source: 2025 GMC Sierra /Sierra Denali 2500HD/3500HD Owner Manual, page 402
