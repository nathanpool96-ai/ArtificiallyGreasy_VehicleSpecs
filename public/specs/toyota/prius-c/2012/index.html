Oil life & reset procedures

Reset the maintenance data (U.S.A. only)

Before you start

  • After the required maintenance is performed according to the maintenance schedule, please reset the maintenance data.

Steps

  1. Vehicles without a smart key system
  2. Switch the display to the odometer (→P. 188) and then turn the engine switch off.
  3. Vehicles with a smart key system
  4. Switch the display to the odometer (→P. 188) and then turn the “POWER” switch off.
  5. Vehicles without a smart key system
  6. Turn the engine switch to the “ON” position. The multi-information display's initial screen will be displayed for a few seconds. While the initial screen is being displayed, press the “TRIP” steering switch (→P. 186) 3 times, pressing and holding the switch on the third time.
  7. Vehicles with a smart key system
  8. Turn the “POWER” switch to ON mode. The multi-information dis- play's initial screen will be displayed for a few seconds. While the initial screen is being displayed, press the “TRIP” steering switch (→P. 186) 3 times, pressing and holding the switch on the third time.
  9. Maintenance and care
  10. Continue to press and hold the button until the trip meter displays “000000”.

You’ll know it worked: Continue to press and hold the button until the trip meter displays “000000”.

Source: Toyota 2012 Prius C Owners Manual (OM52B58U), page 349
