Oil life & reset procedures

Reset the maintenance data (U.S.A. only)

Vehicles without a smart key system

Applies to: Vehicles without a smart key system

Before you start

  • After the required maintenance is performed according to the maintenance schedule, please reset the maintenance data.

Steps

  1. Switch the display to the trip meter A (→P. 90) and then turn the engine switch to the “LOCK” position.
  2. While pressing the “DISP” button (→P. 90), turn the engine switch to the “ON” position (do not start the engine because reset mode will be can- celed).
  3. Continue to press and hold the button until the trip meter displays “000000”.

You’ll know it worked: Continue to press and hold the button until the trip meter displays “000000”.

Source: Toyota 2013 RAV4 Owners Manual (OM42809U), page 413
