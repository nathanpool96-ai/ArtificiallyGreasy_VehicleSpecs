Oil life & reset procedures

Engine oil life monitor reset

Printing 1, Apr 2015

Steps

  1. Press and release the SETUP button to display OIL LIFE XXX% HOLD RESET = NEW.
  2. Press and hold the RESET button for two seconds and release to reset the oil life to 100%. Super Duty (TFA) Canada/United States of America, enUSA, First Printing On or Off

Source: Owner’s Manual Printing 1 (PDF), page 92
