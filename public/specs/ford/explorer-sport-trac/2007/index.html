Oil life & reset procedures

Engine oil life monitor reset

Printings 3 and 4

Steps

  1. Enter SYSTEM CHECK to display “OIL XXX% RESET IF NEW”.
  2. Press SELECT/RESET control switch to display “HOLD RESET IF NEW OIL”.
  3. Press and hold the SELECT/RESET control switch for more than 2 seconds. Oil life is set to 100% and “OIL CHANGE SET TO 100%” is displayed.

Source: Owner’s Manual Printing 4 (PDF), page 105

Engine oil life monitor reset

Printings 1 and 2

Steps

  1. OIL LIFE RESET
  2. CHARGING SYSTEM
  3. WASHER FLUID
  4. DOOR AJAR
  5. EXTERIOR LAMP
  6. BRAKE SYSTEM
  7. PARK BRAKE
  8. MILES TO EMPTY
  9. FUEL LEVEL LOW (will only display if 50 miles or less to empty) To reset the oil monitoring system to 100% after each oil change [approximately 5,000 miles (8,000 km) or 180 days] perform the following: 1. Enter SYSTEM CHECK to display “HOLD RESET IF NEW OIL”. 2. Press and hold the RESET control switch for more than 2 seconds. Oil life is set to 100% and “OIL LIFE SET TO 100%” is displayed.

Source: Owner’s Manual Printing 2 (PDF), page 69
