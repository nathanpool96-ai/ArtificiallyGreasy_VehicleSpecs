Oil life & reset procedures

Engine oil life system reset

Before you start

  • Reset the system whenever the engine oil is changed so that the system can calculate the next engine oil change.

Steps

  1. Press the MENU button to show Remaining Oil Life on the display. This display shows an estimate of the oil's remaining useful life. If 99% is displayed, that means that 99% of the current oil life remains.
  2. To reset the engine oil life system, press the SET/CLR button while the oil life display is active. After a few seconds, there will be a single chime and the oil life will be reset to 100%.
  3. Lower the cover, slide it into the assembly, then secure with the two screws.

Operating the engine with the air cleaner/filter off can cause you or others to be burned.

when working on the engine and do not drive with the air cleaner/filter off.

If the air cleaner/filter is off, dirt can easily get into the engine, which could damage it.

Source: 2015 Buick Encore Owner Manual, page 10-10
