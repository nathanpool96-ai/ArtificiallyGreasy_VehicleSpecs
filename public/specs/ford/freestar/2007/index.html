Oil life & reset procedures

Engine oil life monitor reset

Steps

  1. Press the SETUP control to access the System Check function.
  2. Press and release the RESET control to display “OIL LIFE XX% HOLD RESET NEW”.
  3. Press and hold the RESET control for 2 seconds to display “IF NEW OIL HOLD RESET”.
  4. Press and hold the RESET control to display “OIL LIFE SET TO 100%”. Your oil life is now reset. To reset the oil monitoring system to your personalized oil life %: 1. Press the SETUP control to access the System Check function. 2007 Freestar (win) Owners Guide (post-2002-fmt) USA (fus)
