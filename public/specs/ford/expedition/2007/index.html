Passenger compartment fuse panel

Where to find it

Passenger compartment fuse panel The fuse panel is located under the right-hand side of the instrument panel.

To remove the trim panel for access to the fuse box, pull the panel toward you and swing it out away from the side and remove it.

Source: Owner’s Manual Printing 1 (PDF), page 244
